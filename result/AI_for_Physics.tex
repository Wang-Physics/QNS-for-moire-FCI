\title{Continuum multiband exact diagonalization of twisted MoTe$_2$\\
\large An ED benchmark for CDW and fractional Chern-insulator neural states}
\author{Local numerical benchmark for the Physics-for-AI/QNS programme}
\date{22 August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We establish a symmetry-resolved exact-diagonalization (ED) benchmark and a
continuous-coordinate neural-Bloch experiment for twisted MoTe$_2$.  The
continuum calculation reproduces the one--five-band reference energies, while
one-band 27-cell ED distinguishes the $\nu=1/3$ charge-density-wave tower from
the approximately threefold $\Gamma$ manifold at $\nu=2/3$.  A tracked
three-state calculation gives Chern numbers near zero and two, but complete
all-sector spectral isolation, large-gauge sewing and twist-grid convergence
remain required before assigning a quantized Hall response.

The variational state is one generalized determinant of neural orbitals in the
full plane-wave continuum basis and follows the Luo--Fu Appendix-B
construction.  Four 4,128-walker, 120-update runs compare full momentum mixing
with an exact-$\Gamma$ no-$M$ construction.  Ordinary CG is stopped between
20 and 60 iterations using an explicitly recomputed true residual; unstable
directions are rejected and trigger a temporary larger damping and smaller
learning rate.  Fresh-walker validation selects no $M$ at both fillings.
Independent 4,128-walker, 300-burn-sweep precise validation gives
$E/N_e=-36.78317(1019)$ and $-52.39973(1673)$ meV at $\nu=1/3$ and $2/3$,
remaining 0.6101 and 0.3258 meV per particle above five-band ED.  Full-ratio
five-band-projected 1-RDM estimates give bare-band-1 weights of 96.63(94)\%
and 89.12(86)\%, while direct two-body structure factors preserve the
CDW/FCI contrast.  The current single-seed finite optimization therefore
shows no variational energy advantage and does not support a converged
natural-orbital or topological interpretation.
\end{abstract}

\part{Exact-diagonalization benchmark}

\section{Motivation}

Fractional Chern insulators (FCIs) in moir\'e semiconductors require simultaneous control of strong Coulomb correlations, band mixing and nonuniform Bloch-state quantum geometry.  A quantum neural state (QNS) can reach larger systems and avoid a rigid few-band truncation, but a lower variational energy is not sufficient evidence of the correct phase.  The reference calculation must also reproduce symmetry quantum numbers, the torus ground-state counting, momentum occupation and density correlations.

The present benchmark therefore separates two questions.  First, the $3\times3$ multiband calculation tests the continuum Hamiltonian and the variational convergence from one to five retained bands, directly against Fig.~2 of Ref.~\cite{luo}.  Second, the one-band 27-site calculation repeats the phase diagnostics of Supplementary Fig.~5: a $\Gamma,K,K'$ CDW tower at $\nu=1/3$ versus an approximately threefold $\Gamma$ manifold at $\nu=2/3$~\cite{luo}.  The latter momentum counting is supplemented below by flux spectral flow and a gauge-invariant many-body Chern number.

\section{Continuum model and quantum geometry}

For a fixed spin/valley sector, the single-hole Hamiltonian is
\begin{equation}
H_0=\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\hbar^2(-i\nabla-\bm\kappa_+)^2}{2m^*}+V_1(\bm r)&t(\bm r)\\
t^*(\bm r)&\frac{\hbar^2(-i\nabla-\bm\kappa_-)^2}{2m^*}+V_2(\bm r)
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The lowest moir\'e harmonics and plane-wave convention follow Refs.~\cite{reddy,luo}.  We use
\begin{equation}
(V,w,\phi,m^*)=(11.2\,\mathrm{meV},13.3\,\mathrm{meV},-91^\circ,0.62m_e),
\end{equation}
monolayer lattice constant $a_0=0.352$ nm, and eight radial reciprocal shells.  The resulting Bloch Hamiltonian has 61 plane waves per layer.  Figure~\ref{fig:bands} compares the first ten moir\'e bands at $\theta=2.6^\circ$ and $3.0^\circ$.

For a nondegenerate band $n$, the quantum geometric tensor is evaluated from velocity matrix elements,
\begin{equation}
Q^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}(\bm k)=
\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\langle n|v_\alpha|m\rangle
\langle m|v_\beta|n\rangle}{[\varepsilon_n(\bm k)-\varepsilon_m(\bm k)]^2},
\end{equation}
with $g_{\alpha\beta}=\mathrm{Re}\,Q_{\alpha\beta}$ and
$\Omega=-2\,\mathrm{Im}\,Q_{xy}$.  Bands 1--3 are calculated on a 49-point linear grid in each reciprocal direction, restricted to the hexagonal Brillouin zone, and smoothly triangulated only for visualization.  The shared colorbars retain physical one-dimensional numerical ticks.  Values outside the 1.5--98.5 percentile interval are clipped in the rendering, not in the saved data.

\section{Projected interaction and ED symmetries}

The periodically continued Coulomb interaction is projected into $N_b$ Bloch bands,
\begin{equation}
H=\sum_{n\bm k}\varepsilon_{n\bm k}c^\dagger_{n\bm k}c_{n\bm k}
+\sum_{ab,cd}V_{cd,ab}c_c^\dagger c_d^\dagger c_b c_a,
\end{equation}
where $V(\bm q)=2\pi e^2/(\epsilon|\bm q|)$ and the uniform $\bm q=0$ component is removed by the neutralizing background.  Eight radial reciprocal shells are retained in the form-factor sum.

Charge-$U(1)$ is enforced by fixed hole number, while spin-$U(1)$ is enforced
by restricting to the fully polarized sector.  Translation symmetry first
decomposes the Hilbert space into many-body momentum blocks $\mathcal H_K$.
At generic momentum, a rotation maps $\mathcal H_K$ to the distinct block
$\mathcal H_{C_nK}$, so point-group symmetry is used only to copy spectra
within a momentum orbit.  A further $C_3/C_6$ character decomposition is
available only when $C_nK=K+G$, i.e. when the rotation belongs to the little
group of $K$.

The phase-diagnostic cluster has real-space supercell matrix
\begin{equation}
S_{27}=\begin{pmatrix}3&-3\\3&6\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det S_{27}=27,
\end{equation}
and is therefore the $3\sqrt3\times3\sqrt3$ C6 torus rather than a rectangular $3\times9$ mesh.  Its momentum group is written as $\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_9$, with plot index
\begin{equation}
I_k=k_1+N_1k_2,\qquad N_1=3,\qquad I_k=0,\ldots,26.
\end{equation}
In this convention $\Gamma,K',K$ occur at $I_k=0,9,18$.  The one-band Hilbert space contains $\binom{27}{9}=4{,}686{,}825$ states at $\nu=1/3$.  At $\nu=2/3$, an exactly verified particle--hole transformation maps 18 particles to 9 holes without changing the spectrum.

\begin{equation}
(E_\Gamma,E_K,E_{K'})-E_{\rm GS}=(0,0.573,0.573)\ \mathrm{meV}.
\end{equation}
The next state is $1.476$ meV above the top of this manifold.  The $K,K'$ states fold to $\Gamma$ in the Brillouin zone of the tripled CDW unit cell, matching the interpretation of Ref.~\cite{luo}.

\begin{equation}
(E_{\Gamma,0},E_{\Gamma,1},E_{\Gamma,2})-E_{\rm GS}

\end{equation}
Thus the finite-size threefold splitting is small but explicitly nonzero.  The gap between the highest manifold state and the next global level is $3.482$ meV.  This reproduces the torus counting required for the $\nu=2/3$ FCI/FQAH state.

\section{Twisted-boundary spectra and many-body Berry curvature}

We impose boundary phases $\bm\phi=(\phi_1,\phi_2)$ on the 27-cell torus.  In the common periodic gauge, all cluster momenta are shifted by
\begin{equation}
\delta\bm k=\sum_{a=1}^2\left[S^{-T}\frac{\bm\phi}{2\pi}\right]_a\bm b_a,
\qquad S=\begin{pmatrix}3&-3\\3&6\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The projected interaction is rebuilt at every twist.  Charge-$U(1)$ and translations remain exact, but a generic twist breaks $C_3/C_6$; hence no rotational orbit copying is used in this calculation.

Because the three lowest states are quasi-degenerate, an individual eigenvector Berry phase is gauge dependent.  We instead use the determinant link of the full three-state subspace,
\begin{align}
M_\mu^{ab}(\bm\phi)&=\langle\Psi_a(\bm\phi)|\Psi_b(\bm\phi+\Delta_\mu)|\rangle,\\
U_\mu(\bm\phi)&=\frac{\det M_\mu(\bm\phi)}{|\det M_\mu(\bm\phi)|},\\
\Phi_{12}(\bm\phi)&=\arg\!\left[U_1(\bm\phi)U_2(\bm\phi+\Delta_1)
U_1^*(\bm\phi+\Delta_2)U_2^*(\bm\phi)\right].
\end{align}
The non-Abelian many-body curvature is $\mathcal F_{12}=\Phi_{12}/(\Delta\phi_1\Delta\phi_2)$ and $C_{\rm bundle}=\sum_{\bm\phi}\Phi_{12}/(2\pi)$.  We use a $6\times6$ plaquette grid, while retaining the continuum Bloch-state overlaps in every many-body link.  This single grid is a discrete diagnostic, not a twist-grid convergence study.

Figure~\ref{fig:manybodychern} shows the two axial flux spectra and the curvature map.  For $\nu=1/3$, the bundle contains the lowest states from $\Gamma,K,K^{\prime}$.  Its Chern number is
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm bundle}^{1/3}=9.3\times10^{-7}\simeq0,
\end{equation}
and the smallest sampled separation between the three-state manifold and the nearest state in these three sectors is $1.476$ meV.  For $\nu=2/3$, the three states all belong to $\Gamma$ and
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm tracked}^{2/3}=1.99999975\simeq2.
\end{equation}
If this tracked subspace is subsequently shown to be the globally isolated
ground-state bundle, the conditional Hall response is
$\sigma_{xy}=C_{\rm tracked}e^2/(3h)=2e^2/(3h)$.

value is 0.958 at $\nu=2/3$, and the minimum tracked-sector overlap is 0.964

they do not prove isolation from the complete spectrum.  Missing checks are

\section{ED benchmark protocol for QNS}

The validated workflow is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item solve the plane-wave continuum Hamiltonian and verify shell convergence;
\item calculate band dispersion, Berry curvature and quantum metric on the same Bloch convention;
\item project the neutral-background Coulomb interaction with reciprocal-vector form factors;
\item fix charge-$U(1)$ and spin-$U(1)$, then block by translation; use point-group orbit equivalence at generic $K$, and split by $C_3/C_6$ character only when the rotation belongs to the little group of $K$;
\item use sparse Hermitian Lanczos independently in each required sector;
\item compare energies, sector counting, normalized band weights, $n(\bm k)$ and $S(\bm q)$;
\item insert boundary flux, track the isolated ground manifold and integrate its non-Abelian Berry curvature;
\item only then benchmark the QNS energy, variance and state overlaps against ED.
\end{enumerate}

Numerical regression checks support every stage.  The general C6-supercell interaction agrees with the earlier rectangular $3\times3$ implementation to relative error $3.3\times10^{-16}$.  Direct electron and particle--hole spectra agree sector by sector to $3.4\times10^{-13}$ meV.  The finite plane-wave cutoff produces a maximum one-body C6 mismatch of $8.0\times10^{-6}$ meV, and the pre-symmetrization interaction Hermiticity error is $7.8\times10^{-9}$.  The current complete 51-test regression suite passes; the topology outputs additionally record link conditioning, Lanczos residuals and interaction Hermiticity at every twist.
